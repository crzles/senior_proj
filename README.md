Title: Supplement/Medication Tracker

Team Members: Leslie Cruz (leslie.cruz02@utrgv.edu), Stephanie Gutierrez (stephanie.gutierrez03@utrgv.edu ), Destiny Nava (destiny.nava01@utrgv.edu), Mia Wright (mia.wright01@utrgv.edu)

Faculty Adviser: Sonya Cirlos

Problem/Opportunity Statement: This application will assist individuals who are on short-term and long-term medication/supplements. Helps track their dosage schedule and allow them to register symptoms that arise during treatment.

Background: There are many individuals of different ages around us who struggle with health problems that may or may not be visible to everyone else. They may already feel overwhelmed every day with their daily duties of taking medications and tracking symptoms. Many keep a health diary to keep track of their symptoms or use alarms as reminders, but these tools can easily be forgotten or lost. This is our motive behind our project idea; we want to create a tool that will help ease the user, making it easy for them to navigate and use daily.

Proposed Solution: Our team proposes to create a Supplement/Medication Tracker application that will help users keep track of their medications and supplements in one place. The application will allow users to add their medications or supplements, enter the dosage and schedule, and receive reminders when it is time to take them. Users will also be able to record symptoms they experience while taking their medications or supplements. This will give users an organized way to keep track of their treatment without having to rely on separate apps, alarms, or a physical health diary.

Another feature of the application will be an interaction checker. Users will be able to select medications or supplements from their list, and the system will check them against our own interaction dataset. If a possible interaction is found, the application will notify the user. The application will also include a pharmacy locator using the Google Places API. This will allow users to find nearby pharmacies when they need to refill a medication. These features will be connected to the user's account so their information can be managed in one place.
